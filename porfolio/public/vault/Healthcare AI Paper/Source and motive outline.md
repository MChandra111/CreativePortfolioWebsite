"Our analysis identifies three mutually reinforcing tensions: conceptual fragmentation regarding the definition of ‘agentic’; an autonomy contradiction where commercial promises exceed operational reality; **and an evaluation blind spot that prioritizes technical benchmarks over sociotechnical safety**." - Source 1

Most papers in this space either: Survey existing deployments (survey papers), propose TECHNICAL benchmarks (Called out as an issue in Source 1), or call very abstractly for 'more governance'. What I'd like to do is:
1) Name a specific problem - "The autonomy contradiction". It's identified in literature (Source 1), but isn't formally addressed
2) Propose a named, evaluation framework/instrument that directly addresses the gaps
3) Apply this framework retroactively to real documented cases (Utah Doctronic prescription pilot)
4) Ask for empirical validation

The five dimensions:

| Dimension                  | Plain terms                                                                                                                                                             | Source   |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Autonomy Calibration       | Does the actual autonomy match its marketed autonomy? What level of human oversight is necessary?                                                                       | Source 1 |
| Accountability Mapping     | Is there a named human owner for every action the agent takes? Are 'orphan agents', or those with no clear owner, present?                                              | Source 6 |
| Failure Transparency       | When the agent fails, can a supervising clinician understand why? Is the reasoning accessible and auditable by non-developers?                                          | Source 5 |
| Liability Distribution     | Is legal accountability contractually allocated BEFORE deployment - not discovered after harm occurs?                                                                   | Source 8 |
| Clinician Consent Adequacy | Was the supervising clinician meaningfully and accurately informed about the system's actual capabilities AND limitations before oversight responsibility was assigned? | Gap      |
