Related: [[Cybersecurity]]
Also known as the AIC Triad. Often depicted as a triangle.
## **Confidentiality**

Certain information should only be known to certain people. Prevent unauthorized information disclosure.

Methods: Encryption, Access Controls, 2FA

## **Integrity**

Data is stored and transferred as intended. Any modification to the data would be identified.

Methods: Hashing, Digital Signatures, Certificates, [[Non-Repudiation]]

## **Availability**

Information is accessible to authorized users. Always at your fingertips.

Methods: Redundancy, Fault Tolerance, Patching
