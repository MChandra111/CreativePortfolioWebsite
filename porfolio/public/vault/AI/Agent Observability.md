
[[AI]] Agent Observability is the practice of monitoring, tracing, and understanding the behavior and performance of AI agents across their lifecycle.

Combines traditional observability concepts:
- Metrics
- Events
- Logs
- Traces
With AI-specific telemetry:
- Token Usage
- [[Tool Call]]s
- [[Model Inference]]s
- Decision Paths

We use [[LangSmith]] in ApplyDev for Tracing.
### **Value**

1) End-To-End Visibility
2) Security & Compliance
3) Cross-Platform Support
4) Operational Efficiency
5) Root-Cause Analysis
