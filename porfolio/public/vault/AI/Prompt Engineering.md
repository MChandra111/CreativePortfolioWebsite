
Prompt Engineering is the process of improving a prompt or outlining a specific task given to a [[Generative AI]] tool to complete.

It involves making and refining requests to improve the accuracy, specificity, and usefulness of an [[AI]]'s outputs.

It is important for **AI Engineers** to create better services, such as chatbots, that can handle complex tasks like providing customer service or generating legal contracts. When in larger [[Agentic AI]] or [[Multi-Agent Systems]], each prompt must be carefully engineered to avoid conflict and reach the desired outcome with minimal wasted compute.

It is quite simple, and each model is slightly different:

1) Express your query as clearly as possible. No vagueness.
2) Experiment to come up with best practices.
3) Follow up with instructions or questions
4) Play with different [[Prompting Technique]]s
