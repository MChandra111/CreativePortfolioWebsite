
There are **4 big model families**, each designed by a different company, each with their own strengths and weaknesses.

They are:
1) **ChatGPT** -> Designed by OpenAI. A generalist - fast, creative, and supports a ton of plugins and integrations.
2) **Gemini** -> Designed by Google DeepMind. Emphasizes multimodality, handles not just text but also images, audio, and even video.
3) **Claude** -> Designed by Anthropic. Prioritizes careful reasoning and safety, strong candidate for structured and sensitive tasks.
4) **LLaMA** -> Designed by Meta. Open-sources, allows organizations to fine-tune and deploy models in ways proprietary systems don't.

ChatGPT is often chosen for **creative writing**, conversation, and summarization.

Gemini is ideal for long documents and **research-intensive work**.

Claude excels at step-by-step reasoning, **coding**, and tasks requiring logical precision.

LLaMA 4 brings long context windows and multilingual capabilities, and with the added flexibility of **open-source deployment**
