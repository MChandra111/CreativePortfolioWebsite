
[[AI]] Agent Orchestration refers to the process of coordinating multiple specialized AI agents within a single system, a [[Multi-Agent Systems]], to achieve shared objectives.

There are fundamentally **2** types of AI that we need to understand:
### **[[Generative AI]]**
### **[[Agentic AI]]**

The highest level of the AI progression continuum involves AI Agents, which operate autonomously. They are specialized, and each one is optimized for **a particular function**.

AI Agent Orchestration is akin to a **digital symphony**. Coordination is crucial and it is all orchestrated by a Central AI or a framework.

---
## **Types of Orchestration**

### **Centralized Orchestration**

A single AI orchestrator agent acts as the "brain". Directs all the other agents, assigns tasks, and makes final decisions. Ensures consistency, control, and predictable workflow.
### **Decentralized Orchestration**

Shifts away from a single, controlling entity. Allows MAS to function through direct communication and collaboration. Agents make independent decisions and reach a consensus as a group.
### **Hierarchical Orchestration**

AI Agents are arranged in layers. Higher-level orchestrators oversee and manager lower-level agents. There's a balance between control and task-specific execution.
### **Federated Orchestration**

Collaboration between independent AI agents or separate organizations. Allows them to work together without fully sharing data or relinquishing control over their individual systems. Useful when privacy, security, or regulatory constraints prevent unrestricted data sharing, such as in healthcare, banking, or cross-company collaborations.

## **Process**

1) Assessment and planning
2) Selection of specialized AI agents
3) Orchestration framework implementation
4) Agent selection and assignment
5) Workflow coordination and execution
6) Data sharing and context management
7) Continuous optimization and learning
