
Zero-Shot Prompting -> [[Generative AI]] is given a direct instruction or asked a question without being provided additional information. Best used for simple tasks.

Few-Shot Prompting -> Supplying the [[Generative AI]] with some examples to help guide its output. More suitable for complex tasks.

Chain-Of-Thought (CoT) Prompting -> Helps improve an LLM's output by breaking down complex reasoning into intermediate steps. Similar to [[ReAct Prompting]]

Prompt Chaining -> Prompter splits a complex task into smaller (and easier) subtasks, then uses the GenAI's outputs to accomplish the overarching task. Improves reliability and consistency for some of the most complicated tasks.
