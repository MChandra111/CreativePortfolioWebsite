
In [[AI]] models, especially [[Large Language Models (LLMs)]], **System Prompts** and **User Prompts** work together to shape how the AI responds.

A **System Prompt** is an AI's job description. It is set by the developers or the application, and defines the AI's role, tone, expertise, and rules for the interaction with the user. These instructions stay consistent across multiple conversations.

A **User Prompt** is the specific instructions or questions given by the **user** interacting with the AI.

A System Prompt must clearly define role, tone, etc., and provide relevant background [[Context]].

A User Prompt must be clear about what to include or avoid and use natural language or provide examples/desired formats.
