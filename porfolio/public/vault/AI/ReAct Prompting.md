
ReAct (Reasoning + Acting) prompting is a [[Prompting Technique]] where an [[AI]] combines step-by-step reasoning along with actions. This allows it to think through a problem and take steps to solve it.

Workflow:
1) Reason - Think about the problem and decide what to do
2) Act - Take action based on your own reasoning
3) Observe - Observe the result of the action & get feedback
4) Repeat - Use the new information to reason again & repeat the cycle

### **Value**

Combining Reasoning & Action means the AI can think about a problem and take action in real time. It can adjust its actions based on new information, like a human would.

Sequential steps means the AI can figure out what it has so far and move forward with just that. This keeps the process moving and the reasoning always builds on the previous step.

The AI can make decisions at each step and doesn't have to wait until the end to act. It continuously adjusts its approach.
