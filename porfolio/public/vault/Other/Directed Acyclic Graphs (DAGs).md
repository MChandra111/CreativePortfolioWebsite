
Simply speaking, a graph with no directed cycles. Each edge or node has a **direction**. It points to the next edge or node. There is a one-way relationship or dependency between the nodes.

"Acyclic" refers to the fact that there are no cycles or closed loops. You cannot traverse a sequence of directed edges and return to the same node.

[[LangGraph]] graphs tend to be DAGs.
